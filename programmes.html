Our Programmes

Cultivating growth, knowledge, and community across generations.

🏡 Elderly Support

Care & Wellness

Providing holistic support for community members aged 60 and above. Our programme integrates horticulture therapy, regular health screenings, and wellness activities tailored to maintain mobility and mental acuity.

Through daily visits, group activities, and personalised care plans, we ensure every elder in our community feels seen, valued, and supported.

🌱 Youth Development

Empowering the Future

Fostering the next generation of leaders through targeted workshops in leadership, creative writing, and practical income-generation strategies. We aim to equip youth with the tools they need to succeed and give back.

Our intergenerational bridge programme pairs youth mentees with elder mentors, creating powerful bonds of wisdom and energy.

Learn More
📚 Literacy Programmes

Education & Learning

Bridging educational gaps through accessible literacy programs for all ages. We provide foundational reading, writing, and digital literacy skills, ensuring everyone has the opportunity for lifelong learning.

Our facilitators work with both elders and community members to build confidence, communication skills, and independence.
