Who We Are

Rooted in Respect and Heritage

The Bokwidi Old Age Centre (BOAC) is a beacon of hope and support nestled in the heart of the Waterberg District, Limpopo. We are a registered non-profit organisation driven by a profound respect for the generations that paved the way for our present.

Recognising the vulnerabilities that accompany elderly populations — from social isolation to health challenges and economic cause — BOAC was established to provide a continuous stream of care, dignity, and encouragement. We believe that aging should not mean fading into the background, but rather stepping into a respected role of community elder.

📍 Waterberg District Municipality, Limpopo, South Africa
🎯

Our Mission

To enhance the quality of life for the elderly in Bokwidi and surrounding villages through comprehensive care, nutritional support, health advocacy, and programs that foster social inclusion and emotional well-being.

👁️

Our Vision

A society where every elder lives with dignity, is valued for their wisdom, and enjoys their golden years in a supportive, vibrant, and loving community environment.

Our Location

Rooted in Bokwidi Village

Bokwidi Village is in the Limpopo Waterberg District of Limpopo — it is more than just our location — it is our home and our heart. The village is characterised by its strong community bonds and rich cultural history.

Rooted here, BOAC faces challenges with a core to accessibility of healthcare and social services. By centring our operations here, BOAC ensures that vital support reaches those who need it most without uprooting them from the land and place they love.
