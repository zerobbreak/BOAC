The Programme

Fostering Leaders from
Within the Community

Our Youth Development programme bridges the gap between generations. Young people from across the Waterberg District come to BOAC not just to learn — but to connect, grow, and give back.

Through structured workshops in leadership, creative writing, entrepreneurship, and practical life skills, we equip participants with tools that serve them and their communities for a lifetime.

Most importantly, our intergenerational approach pairs youth with our elders — creating irreplaceable bonds of wisdom, mentorship, and mutual respect.
