Your contribution directly impacts the daily lives of the elderly in Bokwidi Village. Every donation helps us provide nutritious meals, essential healthcare support, and a safe, dignified environment for those who guided us.

Why Your Support Matters

🍽️

Daily Nutrition

Providing balanced, culturally appropriate meals to ensure our seniors maintain their health and vitality.

💊

Healthcare Access

Facilitating regular medical check-ups, medication management, and specialised care for age-related conditions.

🌍

Community Programs

Funding social activities, skills workshops, and mental well-being initiatives to prevent isolation.
